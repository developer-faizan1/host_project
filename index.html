ABOUT AL HABBAI

Building Trust,
Delivering Excellence

Since 1993, Al Habbai Contracting, under the leadership of CEO Mr. Ali Al Habbai, has built a reputation for excellence in construction. With decades of experience, ISO-certified quality standards, and an unlimited storey construction license, we proudly deliver exceptional projects across Dubai.

  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified
  • Unlimited Storey Building License

WHY CHOOSE US

Quality Assurance

Safety First

Safety is our highest priority, with strict compliance, regular training, advanced protective measures, and continuous monitoring to ensure a secure working environment for everyone on every project.

Expert Team

Our highly skilled engineers, architects, and construction professionals bring extensive experience, technical expertise, and dedication to successfully deliver projects with precision, quality, and efficiency.

On-Time Delivery
